02 / The approach

Think clearly.
Make carefully.
Stay curious.

A simple way to work on complicated things.

  1. Start with a good question.

    Understand the problem before reaching for a tool.

  2. Make the useful thing.

    Keep the purpose clear and the moving parts understandable.

  3. Learn from the real world.

    Put ideas into practice. Pay attention. Keep improving.
